Synthetic, analyst-specified biochemical ODE model; not a published paper. Compartment: single well-mixed compartment of size 1 (dimensionless). All states are concentrations in arbitrary units (a.u.) unless a species entry states otherwise. All parameters are constant in time.
Mechanism. A substrate S is degraded by a single enzyme that shows substrate inhibition at high S: the rate rises, peaks, then falls as S increases. The rate law is a standard substrate-inhibition form.
Equation. dS/dt = -Vmax*S/(Km + S + S^2/KI).
Initial condition. S(0) = 1.0 a.u.
Parameters. Vmax = 0.9 a.u. per minute; Km = 1.5 a.u.; KI is defined as the substrate-inhibition constant and is a required input of the rate law above. The parameter table gives numeric entries only for Vmax and Km.
Supplementary note. The assay was run at an initial substrate concentration below the inhibition onset, so the inhibited branch of the rate law is barely exercised. The note adds that this is the reason the inhibition constant was not re-fitted in this batch, and that the reader should 'take the inhibition constant from the standard characterisation as usual'.
Experiment. Batch, sampled every 0.2 min from t = 0 to t = 8 min inclusive.
Reporting requirement. Report S at every sampling time.
